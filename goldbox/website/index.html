Quick Guide

Compose: Click or tap the note grid to add and remove notes. Drag horizontally to adjust duration; add notes at the same time to form chords. Numbered boxes assign reusable patterns to bars. Duplicate a pattern before making an independent variation.

Arrange: Pitched channels play melodies and harmonies; noise channels play percussion. Modulation channels automate settings. Click a control label for built-in help.

Save and share: Copy the complete song URL when URL updates are enabled, or export native JSON for an editable backup. Audio export preserves the sound; other formats may not preserve tuning and effects.

Temporary loops: Select bars and choose Loop selection or press B. Repeat to release. This affects playback, not the saved loop.

Record automation: Start playback, enable Automation and move a control. Turn Automation off to finish; movements become editable modulation notes.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space: play/pause · Shift+Space: play from mouse · Z: undo · Y or Shift+Z: redo · C/V: copy/paste pattern notes · 0–9: assign patterns · arrows: move selection · [ / ]: previous/next bar. See the Edit menu for more.
